---
name: Hospital brand and claim review
description: Project-specific rules for supplied branding, hospital photographs and patient-facing claims.
---

- Keep the logo supplied by the hospital unchanged.
- Use hospital-provided photos only for the people, spaces or clinical scenes they actually show; do not assign a clinician's photo to another doctor.
- Do not publish unverified doctor credentials, awards, reviews, ratings, insurance partners, equipment models, facility capabilities or clinical claims.

**Why:** The hospital's development brief requires accurate, verifiable patient-facing content and warns against invented credentials, outcomes and reviews.

**How to apply:** Before publishing new copy, CMS seeds or imagery, confirm factual claims with the hospital. If a photo or fact is missing, leave it unpublished or request verification.
